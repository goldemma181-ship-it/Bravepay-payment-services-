Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy explains what we collect and why. Short version: we collect the minimum needed to fulfill your order, never ask for your social passwords, and never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Order details: the public URL you want the service delivered to, the service chosen, quantity, and your email address.
  • Payment data: handled by Paystack (card details never touch our servers) or your bank transfer narration. We store only the payment status and reference.
  • Basic technical logs: IP-based rate-limiting and security logs, kept to protect the service from abuse.

What we never collect

We never ask for your social-media passwords or account access. No BRAVE page or email should ever request one.

How we use your data

  • To deliver the service you ordered and email you receipts and status updates.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive ordering.
  • For bookkeeping (orders may be exported as CSV records).

Sharing

We share only what is necessary: your email and order reference with our payment processor (Paystack), and delivery details with the team fulfilling your order. We do not sell personal data.

Retention & your rights

Order records are kept for bookkeeping. You can request correction or deletion of your data at the support address on your receipt; we will action reasonable requests.

Cookies & local storage

The site uses no tracking cookies. Your browser may store your admin/tracking details locally on your own device only.
