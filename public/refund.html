Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026. We want every order delivered. If something goes wrong, here is how refunds work.

When you get a refund

  • We cannot start delivery within 72 hours of payment confirmation.
  • We cannot deliver any portion of the ordered service and cancel the order.
  • You ordered against a private or removed page — the order is cancelled.

When refunds don't apply

  • Delivery has started and partially completed (we may offer a partial refund for the undelivered portion).
  • You provided a wrong URL — contact support before delivery starts and we will correct it for free.
  • Drops after delivery: growth naturally fluctuates; contact support and we will review reasonable drop cases.

How refunds are paid

Paystack payments are refunded to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days. Bank-transfer payments are refunded to the transferring account. Refunds are processed by our team once approved.

How to request one

Reply to your order receipt email or contact support with your order ID (BRV-…). We respond within 48 hours.
